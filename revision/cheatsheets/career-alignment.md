# Career Alignment Cheatsheet

- Primary: storage/workload control planes, lifecycle, reliability, performance.
- Complementary: AI infrastructure only after repeated role and project evidence.
- Weekly default: six hours; ceiling eight; busy-week floor three to four.
- Validate: role scorecard, interview feedback, project preference, sustainable
  time. Revisit at day 120 and quarterly.
- Keep: one progressive control-plane project and Go-first core.
- Defer: Rust and advanced specialties until a role or project trigger exists.
- Never infer hiring, pay, or team quality from one job posting.
