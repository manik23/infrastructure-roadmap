# Career-validation checkpoint

Use this prompt during the first 120 days and then quarterly. Do not alter
technical progress status from intentions or job titles alone.

1. Review 5 to 10 recent official role descriptions and update
   career/ROLE_SCORECARD.csv with evidence, not assumptions.
2. Identify repeated skills as demonstrated, practiced, gap, or not relevant.
3. Compare the primary path, storage depth, and AI-infrastructure interest
   against actual work content, on-call, location, and sustainability.
4. Review project evidence, interview feedback, and the current skill-gap
   queue. Select one next task with the highest career value.
5. Record what to defer or stop. Do not add a technology merely because it is
   fashionable.
6. Keep the six-hour default and life commitments sustainable. Extend the
   calendar rather than borrowing sleep or family time.

Decision rule: change direction only from repeated role evidence, practitioner
feedback, project preference, interview feedback, or real work opportunities.
